About Us

Your Recovery,
Our Mission

At The Urban Care Physiotherapy Clinic, we believe in a holistic, patient-first approach to rehabilitation. Our expert team combines advanced techniques with compassionate care to help you reclaim your mobility and live pain-free.

Whether you're recovering from an injury, surgery, or managing a chronic condition, we design personalised treatment plans that address the root cause — not just the symptoms.

What We Treat

Our Services

Comprehensive physiotherapy treatments tailored to your unique needs and recovery goals.

🦴

Orthopaedic Physiotherapy

Treatment for bone, joint, and muscle conditions including fractures, arthritis, back pain, and post-surgical rehabilitation.

🧠

Neurological Rehabilitation

Specialised care for stroke recovery, Parkinson's disease, spinal cord injuries, and other neurological conditions.

🏃

Sports Injury Rehab

Targeted recovery programmes for athletes dealing with sprains, ligament tears, and performance-related injuries.

🤸

Manual Therapy

Hands-on techniques including joint mobilisation, soft tissue therapy, and myofascial release for pain relief and flexibility.

⚡

Electrotherapy

Advanced modalities such as TENS, ultrasound therapy, and interferential therapy to accelerate healing and reduce pain.

🧘

Postural & Balance Training

Corrective exercises to improve posture, prevent falls, and enhance everyday functional movement and stability.
